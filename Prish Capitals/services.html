Common Service Inquiries

Answers to help you select the right financial solutions

What documents are needed to file an Income Tax Return (ITR)?
For salaried individuals: Form 16, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, bank statements, and deduction proofs (80C, 80D, home loan interest). For businesses & freelancers: Profit & Loss statement, balance sheet, GST turnover records, Form 26AS, and AIS/TIS summary.
How frequently do I need to file GST returns?
Regular GST-registered businesses must file monthly returns (GSTR-1 for sales outward supply and GSTR-3B for summary return & tax payment). Eligible small taxpayers with turnover up to ₹5 Crore can opt for the quarterly QRMP scheme, plus the annual GSTR-9 return.
Why should my business register under MSME / Udyam?
MSME Udyam registration gives your enterprise legal recognition, enabling priority bank loans at discounted interest rates, exemption from tender fees, government capital subsidies, electricity concessions, and statutory protection against late buyer payments.
