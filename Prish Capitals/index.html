Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers about our financial, GST, and tax consultancy services

What financial and tax consultancy services does Prish Capitals offer in Bangalore?
Prish Capitals provides full-spectrum tax, accounting, and compliance solutions for businesses and individuals across Bangalore and India. Our services include GST registration & return filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9), Income Tax Return (ITR) filing, MSME/Udyam registrations, company incorporation, TDS management, corporate bookkeeping, and tax dispute resolution.

How does Prish Capitals assist businesses with GST registration and monthly compliance?
We manage your entire GST lifecycle: obtaining your new GSTIN number, conducting monthly sales/purchase data reconciliations, claiming maximum Input Tax Credit (ITC), filing monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns on time, preparing annual GSTR-9 audits, and responding to GST department notices with 99%+ accuracy.
Can salaried employees and freelancers file their Income Tax Returns (ITR) with Prish Capitals?
Yes. We handle ITR filing for salaried professionals, freelancers, consultants, traders, and small business owners (ITR-1 through ITR-7). Our tax specialists maximize deductions under Section 80C, 80D, HRA, and home loans while ensuring full compliance with the latest Indian tax laws.
What are the benefits of MSME Udyam registration for small businesses?
MSME / Udyam registration grants small businesses significant advantages including priority collateral-free bank loans, lower interest rates, government tender preferences, electricity tariff subsidies, patent rebates, and legal protection against delayed customer payments under MSMED Act rules.
